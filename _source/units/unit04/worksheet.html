GRAMMAR FOCUS — Present continuous; conjunctions 语法重点 — 现在进行时；连词 p. 26

{{AUDIO:p026-ex09-grammar-focus.mp3|Track 50}}

present continuous · and / but / so

I'm / You're / She's / We're / They're wearing shoes. She's not wearing boots.  OR  She isn't wearing boots.
It's snowing.It's not raining.  OR  It isn't raining.
It's snowing, and it's windy. · It's sunny, but it's cold. · It's windy, so it's very cold.

「正在做某事」= be + 动词-ing。两个部分缺一不可：
She wearing boots.（少了 be）  She is wear boots.（少了 -ing）
中文用「正在」「着」，动词本身不变；英语必须同时改两处。

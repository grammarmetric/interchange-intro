count & noncount · some / any

Count nounsNoncount nouns
an egg → eggs · a potato → potatoesbread · lettuce
Do we need any potatoes?Do we need any lettuce?
Yes. Let's get some.Yes. Let's get some.
No. We don't need any.No. We don't need any.

可数名词可以说「一个、两个」，有复数：an egg / eggs。
不可数名词没有复数，也不能加 a/an：bread、lettuce、rice、cheese。
中文没有这个分别（「一块面包」「一片生菜」都靠量词），所以要一个一个记。
some 用在肯定句，any 用在疑问句和否定句——两者都不管可数不可数。

GRAMMAR FOCUS — Adverbs of frequency 语法重点 — 频度副词 p. 61

{{AUDIO:p061-t7-track-7.mp3|CD 2 · p. 61, second track}}

always · usually · often · sometimes · hardly ever · never

I always / usually / often / sometimes / hardly ever / never eat breakfast. Sometimes I eat breakfast.
Do you ever have fish for breakfast? Yes, I always do. · Sometimes I do. · No, I never do.
100% always → usually → often → sometimes → hardly ever → never 0%

位置很重要：频度副词放在主要动词前面，但放在 be 的后面。
I always eat breakfast.  /  I am always late.
中文的「总是、经常、从不」放在动词前面，这一点和英语一样； 但 sometimes 还可以放句首，其他的一般不行。
另外：never 本身就是否定，不能再加 don't（I don't never eat）。

A Where does the adverb go? A 副词应该放在哪里？

  • 1Do you have a big breakfast? (usually) — Do you usually have a big breakfast? (example)（例）
  • 2On Sunday I have a big breakfast with my friends. (always) — On Sunday I always have a big breakfast with my friends.
  • 3Do you eat breakfast at work on weekdays? (ever) — Do you ever eat breakfast at work on weekdays?
  • 4Yes, I have breakfast at the office restaurant. (sometimes) — Yes, I sometimes have breakfast at the office restaurant.
  • 5No, I eat breakfast at my desk. (hardly ever) — No, I hardly ever eat breakfast at my desk.

B Put the words in order. B 把词排成正确的句子。

  • 1I / have / hardly ever / beef / for lunch — I hardly ever have beef for lunch. (example)（例）
  • 2when I watch TV / I / snacks / eat / never — I never eat snacks when I watch TV.
  • 3eat / for / eggs / breakfast / sometimes / I — I sometimes eat eggs for breakfast. (or Sometimes I eat eggs for breakfast.)
  • 4have / I / dinner / with / usually / family / my — I usually have dinner with my family.

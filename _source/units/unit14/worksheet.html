GRAMMAR FOCUS — Simple past statements; regular verbs 语法重点 — 一般过去时陈述句：规则动词 p. 93

{{AUDIO:p093-t3-track-3.mp3|CD 3 · p. 93, first track}}

simple past · regular verbs

I stayed home. · You watched a TV show.I didn't stay home. · You didn't watch a game.
She exercised on Saturday. · We studied English.She didn't exercise on Sunday. · We didn't study math.
They shopped for groceries.They didn't shop for clothes.   (didn't = did not)

Spelling: stay → stayed · watch → watched · exercise → exercised · study → studied · shop → shopped

规则动词过去式：动词 + -ed，所有人称都一样，不像现在时还要分 he/she。
拼写四条：一般加 -ed；已经以 e 结尾只加 -d；辅音 + y 改成 -ied； 单音节「辅音+元音+辅音」要双写末尾字母再加 -ed（shop → shopped）。
否定：didn't + 动词原形——注意 didn't 后面动词要变回原形， 不能说 didn't stayed。这是最常见的错误。
中文的「了」不随人称变化，英语的 -ed 也不随人称变化，这一点反而好记。

    PRONUNCIATION — Simple past -ed endings. Listen and practise. 语音 —《过去式 -ed 的读音》。先听录音跟读。 p. 93

    {{AUDIO:p093-t4-track-4-a.mp3|CD 3 · p. 93, second track}}

    /t/ worked · watched    /d/ cleaned · stayed    /ɪd/ wanted · visited

    同样写作 -ed，读音有三种，取决于前面那个音：
    前面是清辅音（k、ch、p、s、f、sh）→ 读 /t/：worked、watched、shopped
    前面是浊音或元音（n、l、v、z、r 等）→ 读 /d/：cleaned、stayed、listened
    前面是 t 或 d → 多出一个音节，读 /ɪd/：wanted、visited、decided
    只有第三种会让单词多一个音节，其余两种音节数不变——中国学生常常多读一个音。

    simple past · irregular verbs

    I did my homework. / I didn't do the laundry.You got up at noon. / You didn't get up at 8:00.
    She went to the bookstore. / She didn't go to the library.We met our classmates. / We didn't meet our teacher.
    You came home late. / You didn't come home early.They had a barbecue. / They didn't have a picnic.

    不规则动词的过去式不加 -ed，形式要一个一个背：go→went、have→had、do→did。
    但否定句里它们全都变回原形：didn't go、didn't have、didn't do—— 所以背不规则形式只在肯定句里用得上。

    {{AUDIO:p095-t8-track-8.mp3|CD 3 · p. 95, second track}}

    simple past yes/no questions

    Did you have a good summer?
    Yes, I did. I had a great summer.    		Did Martin like his vacation?
    Yes, he did. He liked it a lot.
    Did you go surfing?
    No, I didn't. I went swimming.    		Did Martin and his sister go to Montreal?
    No, they didn't. They went to Sydney.

    问句结构：Did + 主语 + 动词原形？——过去的意思由 Did 表示， 所以后面的动词一定用原形：Did you go？不是 Did you went。
    短答：Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.——不管什么人称都用 did / didn't。
    但回答里如果说完整句子，动词就要变过去式：No, I didn't. I went swimming.

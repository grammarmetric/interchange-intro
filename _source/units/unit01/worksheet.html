{{AUDIO:p006-ex10-pronunciation.mp3|Track 12}}

When a word ends in a consonant and the next word begins with a vowel (a, e, i, o, u), the two sounds join.

前一个词以辅音结尾、后一个词以元音（a, e, i, o, u）开头时，这两个音会连起来读。

I'm‿Isabella.   She's‿over there.   You're‿in my class.

Which two words join together? 哪两个词会连读？

  • 1I'm Isabella. — I'm + Isabella
  • 2She's over there. — She's + over
  • 3You're in my class. — You're + in

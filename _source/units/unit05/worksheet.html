CONVERSATION — It's two o'clock in the morning! Listen and practise. 对话 —《现在是凌晨两点！》先听录音跟读。 p. 30

AmarHello?喂？

BrianHi, Amar! This is Brian. I'm calling from New York.嗨，阿马尔！我是布莱恩，从纽约打给你的。

AmarBrian? Wait. . . . Where are you?布莱恩？等等……你在哪儿？

BrianI'm home on vacation, remember? I'm calling about the soccer game. Great game!我放假回家了，记得吗？我是为了那场足球赛打给你的。太精彩了！

AmarOh, that's good. But what time is it there?哦，那挺好。可你那边几点了？

BrianIt's 2:00 p.m. And it's two o'clock in Australia, too. Right?下午两点。澳大利亚也是两点吧？

AmarThat's right — it's two o'clock in the morning!没错——是凌晨两点！

Brian2:00 a.m.? Oh, of course! I'm really sorry.凌晨两点？哎呀，是啊！真对不起。

AmarThat's OK. Congratulations on the game!没关系。恭喜球队赢球！

  • 1 Brian is calling from New York.布莱恩是从纽约打来的。
    True
  • 2 It is 2:00 in the afternoon for Amar.对阿马尔来说现在是下午两点。
    False — it's 2:00 a.m., the middle of the night
  • 3 Both cities show two o'clock, but not the same two o'clock. 两个城市都是「两点」，但不是同一个两点。
    True — 2:00 p.m. and 2:00 a.m.
  • 4 Amar is angry with Brian.阿马尔生布莱恩的气了。
    False — “That's OK.”

GRAMMAR FOCUS — What time is it? 语法重点 —《现在几点？》 p. 31

{{AUDIO:p031-ex03-grammar-focus.mp3|Track 56}}
{{IMG:ex03-clocks|Six clock faces showing two o'clock, two-oh-five, two-fifteen, two-thirty, two-forty and two forty-five}}

英语说时间有两套办法：直接读数字（two-fifteen）和 after / to（a quarter after two、twenty to three）。 过了半点就用 to 说「差多少到下一个小时」——这一点和中文的「差一刻三点」是一样的想法。

A What time is it? A 现在几点？

{{IMG:ex03-watches|Six clocks and watches showing different times}}
  • 1 It's ten-ten. / It's ten after ten.
  • 2 It's six-oh-one. / It's one minute after six.
  • 3 It's five-fifty. / It's ten to six.
  • 4 It's eight-thirty.
  • 5 It's nine-oh-five. / It's five after nine.
  • 6 It's twelve forty-five. / It's a quarter to one.

B Is it a.m. or p.m.? Say each time a different way. B 是上午还是下午？把每个时间换一种说法。

{{AUDIO:p031-ex03-grammar-focus-a.mp3|Track 57}}
{{IMG:ex03-ampm|The same bridge at six times of day, from morning to midnight}}
  • 1It's eight o'clock in the morning. — It's 8:00 a.m. (example)（例）
  • 2It's three o'clock in the afternoon. — It's 3:00 p.m.
  • 3It's six o'clock in the evening. — It's 6:00 p.m.
  • 4It's twelve o'clock at night. — It's midnight.
  • 5It's 10:00 a.m. — It's ten (o'clock) in the morning.
  • 6It's 4:00 p.m. — It's four (o'clock) in the afternoon.
  • 7It's 7:00 p.m. — It's seven (o'clock) in the evening.
  • 8It's 12:00 p.m. — It's noon.

CONVERSATION — What are you doing? Listen and practise. 对话 —《你在做什么？》先听录音跟读。 p. 32

{{AUDIO:p032-ex05-conversation.mp3|Track 60}}

JayHey, Kate!嘿，凯特！

KateWhat are you doing?你在做什么？

JayI'm cooking.我在做饭。

KateI know, but why are you cooking now? It's three o'clock in the morning!我看到了，可你为什么这个点做饭？现在是凌晨三点！

JayI'm sorry, but I'm really hungry.对不起，可我真的饿了。

KateHmm . . . What are you making?嗯……你在做什么吃的？

KateWith tomato sauce?配番茄酱吗？

    True

06

PRONUNCIATION — Rising and falling intonation. Listen and notice. 语音 —《升调和降调》。听录音，注意语调。 p. 32

{{AUDIO:p032-ex06-pronunciation-a.mp3|Track 61}}

Yes/no questions rise: Is he cooking? ↗  ·  Wh- questions fall: What's he making? ↘

一般疑问句（yes/no）语调上升：Is he cooking? ↗
特殊疑问句（what、where、who…）语调下降：What's he making? ↘
汉语靠「吗」和语气词表达疑问，英语很多时候只靠语调，所以这一点必须练。

B Listen. Does the question rise or fall? B 听录音，判断是升调还是降调。

{{AUDIO:p032-ex06-pronunciation-b.mp3|Track 62}}
  • 1 rising ↗
  • 2 falling ↘
  • 3 falling ↘
  • 4 rising ↗
  • 5 falling ↘
  • 6 rising ↗

present continuous Wh-questions · spelling

What's Daniel doing?He's sleeping right now.
What are Lya and Erin doing?They're having breakfast.
sleep → sleeping · get → getting (+t) · have → having (−e)

疑问词 + be + 主语 + 动词-ing。三样东西缺一不可。
拼写要注意：短元音结尾的单音节动词要双写末尾字母（get → getting）， 以不发音的 e 结尾的要去 e（have → having）。

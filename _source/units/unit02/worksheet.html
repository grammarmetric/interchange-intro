Yes/No and where questions with be

Is this your wallet?Where's your wallet? — It's in my pocket.
Yes, it is. / No, it's not.Where are my keys? — They're on the table.
Are these your keys?
Yes, they are. / No, they're not.

一、提问要把 be 提到最前面。中文加「吗」语序不变，英语要说 Is this your wallet?

三、肯定的简短回答不缩写：Yes, it is.（不说 Yes, it's.）否定可以：No, it's not.

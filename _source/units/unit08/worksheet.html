GRAMMAR FOCUS — Simple present Wh-questions 语法重点 — 一般现在时的特殊疑问句 p. 52

{{AUDIO:p052-t4-track-4.mp3|CD 2 · p. 52, first track}}

Wh-word + do/does + subject + verb

Where do you work?In a hospital.
What do you do?I'm a doctor.
How do you like it?I really like it.
Where does he work?In a hotel.
What does he do?He's a manager.
How does he like it?It's OK.

三个问句意思完全不同，别混：
Where do you work? 问地方（在哪儿上班）
What do you do? 问职业（做什么工作）—— 不是问「你在干什么」
How do you like it? 问感受（觉得怎么样）
语序永远是：疑问词 + do/does + 主语 + 动词原形。

PRONUNCIATION — Reduction of do. Listen and notice. 语音 —《do 的弱读》。听录音，注意 do 是怎么读轻的。 p. 52

{{AUDIO:p052-t5-track-5.mp3|CD 2 · p. 52, second track}}

Where do you work? · What do you do? · Where do they work? · What do they do?

In natural speech do you sounds like “d'you” and do they like “d'they”. The verb after it keeps its full sound.

自然语速下，do you 听起来像「d'you」，do they 像「d'they」——do 读得又轻又短。
汉语每个字都读得清清楚楚，英语的功能词却常常「吞掉」。听不出 do，往往就听不懂整句问话。

  • 1In Where do you work?, the word do is — short and weak
  • 2do you sounds most like — d'you
  • 3Which word is stressed in What do you do? — the last do — the main verb
  • 4Reduction happens to — helping words like do

GRAMMAR FOCUS — Placement of adjectives 语法重点 — 形容词的位置 p. 54

{{AUDIO:p054-t9-track-9.mp3|CD 2 · p. 54}}

be + adjective  ·  adjective + noun

A doctor's job is stressful.A doctor has a stressful job.
A firefighter's job is dangerous.A firefighter has a dangerous job.

同一个意思，两种说法：形容词可以放在 be 后面，也可以放在名词前面。
放名词前面的时候别忘了冠词：a stressful job（不能说 stressful a job）。
中文说「压力大的工作」，语序和英语一致，但英语多了一个 a。

A Write each sentence a different way. A 换一种说法，表达同样的意思。

  • 1A photographer's job is interesting. — A photographer has an interesting job. (example)（例）
  • 2A pilot's job is exciting. — A pilot has an exciting job.
  • 3A teacher's job is stressful. — A teacher has a stressful job.
  • 4A cashier has a boring job. — A cashier's job is boring.
  • 5An accountant has a difficult job. — An accountant's job is difficult.
  • 6A receptionist has an easy job. — A receptionist's job is easy.

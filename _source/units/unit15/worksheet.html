{{AUDIO:p101-t3-track-3.mp3|CD 3 · p. 101}}

past of be

be 的过去式只有两个形式：was（I / he / she / it）和 were（you / we / they）。
否定：wasn't = was not，weren't = were not。
疑问句把 was/were 提到主语前面：Were you…？ Was she…？
短答用 Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.——不要加 did， be 动词的句子从来不用 do/did。这是最容易和上一单元混淆的地方。

PRONUNCIATION — Negative contractions. Listen and practise. 语音 —《否定缩写》。先听录音跟读。 p. 102

{{AUDIO:p102-t4-track-4-a.mp3|CD 3 · p. 102, first track}}

one syllable: aren't · don't · weren't · can't    two syllables: isn't · doesn't · wasn't · didn't

否定缩写有的读一个音节，有的读两个音节——这决定了句子的节奏。
一个音节：aren't /ɑːrnt/、don't /doʊnt/、weren't /wɜːrnt/、can't /kænt/
两个音节：isn't /ˈɪz-ənt/、doesn't /ˈdʌz-ənt/、wasn't /ˈwʌz-ənt/、didn't /ˈdɪd-ənt/
规律：n't 前面是 z 或 d 音时要多出一个音节；其他情况直接接上去。

Wh-questions with did, was and were

Where did you grow up?I grew up in New York.
What did your father do there?He worked in a department store.
Why did you become a hairstylist?Because I needed the money.
Where were you born?I was born in Brooklyn.
How old were you in 2008?I was eighteen.
What was your major in college?Photography. I was a photographer for five years.

两个句型，选哪一个看动词是不是 be：
普通动词 → 疑问词 + did + 主语 + 动词原形？（Where did you grow up?）
be 动词 → 疑问词 + was/were + 主语？（Where were you born?）
千万别把两个混在一起说成 Where did you were born?——一个句子里只能有一个「过去时标记」。

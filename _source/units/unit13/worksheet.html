复合名词（两个词合起来表示一样东西）的重音几乎总是落在第一个词上—— 课本上八个词的黑点全在第一个词上面。
所以 GAS station 不是 gas STAtion；POST office 不是 post OFfice。
重音位置错了，母语者常常听不懂——这比发音本身还重要。

  • 1In gas station, the stress is on — gas — the first word
  • 2In post office, the stress is on — post
  • 3In compound nouns, the stress normally falls on — the first word

prepositions of place

The department store is on Third Avenue.It's next to the bank.
It's on the corner of Third and Market.The bank is between the department store and the restaurant.
It's across from the park. 

on ＋ 街道名 ＝ 在某条街上（on Third Avenue）
on the corner of A and B ＝ 在 A 街和 B 街的路口
across from ＝ 在……对面（隔着一条街）
next to ＝ 紧挨着、旁边
between A and B ＝ 在 A 和 B 中间——注意中间要用 and，不能用逗号。

GRAMMAR FOCUS — Directions 语法重点 — 指路 p. 90

{{AUDIO:p090-t9-track-9.mp3|CD 3 · p. 90}}

giving directions

How do I get to Rockefeller Center?How can I get to the New York Public Library?
Walk up / Go up Fifth Avenue.Walk down / Go down Fifth Avenue.
Turn left on 49th Street.Turn right on 42nd Street.
It's on the right.It's on the left.

问路两种说法通用：How do I get to…? / How can I get to…?
walk up / go up ＝ 往街道号码变大的方向走；walk down / go down ＝ 往号码变小的方向走。 跟「上坡下坡」没关系。
turn left / right on + 街名（介词用 on）；到了地方说 It's on the left / right。
指路全用祈使句，跟第 12 单元一样：动词原形开头，不要主语。

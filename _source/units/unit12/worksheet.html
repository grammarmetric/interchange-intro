GRAMMAR FOCUS — Have + noun; feel + adjective 语法重点 — have + 名词；feel + 形容词 p. 79

{{AUDIO:p079-t3-track-3.mp3|CD 3 · p. 79, second track}}

have + noun · feel + adjective

What's the matter? · What's wrong?How are you? · How do you feel?
I have a stomachache.I feel sick.
I have a headache.I feel better.
I have the flu.I don't feel well.

Negative adjectives: horrible · awful · terrible · miserable
Positive adjectives: fine · great · terrific · fantastic

说病名用 have + 名词：I have a headache.（我头疼）
说感觉用 feel + 形容词：I feel sick.（我难受）
中文都是「我头疼／我难受」，看不出区别；英语要看后面接的是名词还是形容词。
病名前多半要加 a/an（a headache、an earache），但 the flu 用 the。
well 在这里是「身体好」，是形容词：I don't feel well. 不要说 I don't feel good。

A Listen and practise. “He has a backache.” A 听录音跟读十种常见病症。

{{AUDIO:p079-t3-track-3-a.mp3|CD 3 · p. 79, the ailments}}
{{IMG:ex03-ailments|Ten ailments: a backache, an earache, a headache, a stomachache, a toothache, a cold, a cough, a fever, the flu and a sore throat}}

Choose have or feel. 选 have 还是 feel。

  • 1I a toothache. have — a toothache is a noun
  • 2I terrible. feel — terrible is an adjective
  • 3She the flu. has

  • 5I don't well. feel — well is an adjective here

  • 2Your ear hurts. — an earache — an, because earache starts with a vowel
  • 3Your temperature is very high. — a fever

PRONUNCIATION — Sentence intonation. Listen and practise. 语音 —《句子语调》。先听录音跟读。 p. 81

{{AUDIO:p081-t8-track-8.mp3|CD 3 · p. 81, third track}}

Take these pills. ↘   Drink some tea. ↘   Try to relax. ↘
Don't take cough syrup. ↘   Don't drink coffee. ↘   Don't work too hard. ↘

祈使句（命令和建议）句尾语调往下降——课本上六句的箭头全是向下的。
降调听起来是「肯定、给建议」；如果句尾升上去，听起来就成了疑问或者不确定。
这跟一般疑问句（Do you…? ↗ 升调）正好相反。

  • 1In Take these pills. the voice at the end goes — down
  • 2In Don't drink coffee. the voice at the end goes — down — negative imperatives fall too
  • 3Imperatives — orders and advice — normally use — falling intonation

GRAMMAR FOCUS — Imperatives 语法重点 — 祈使句 p. 82

{{AUDIO:p082-t9-track-9.mp3|CD 3 · p. 82}}

imperatives

Get some rest.Don't stay up late.
Drink lots of juice.Don't drink soda.
Take one pill every evening.Don't work too hard.

祈使句用来给建议或下指令：不要主语，直接用动词原形开头。
肯定：动词原形 + 其他 → Get some rest.
否定：Don't + 动词原形 → Don't stay up late.
跟中文很像（「多休息」「别熬夜」），主要记住否定一律用 Don't，不管对谁说。

at home · work · school · the mall · the library · the beach

这三个介词没有简单规律，只能整块记住：
at 多用在「场所／机构」——at work（在上班）、at school（在上学）
in 多用在「里面／状态」——in bed（在床上）、in a meeting（在开会）
on 多用在「外出的活动」——on vacation（在休假）、on a trip（出差旅行）
注意 in the hospital（住院）和 at the hospital（在医院这个地方）意思不同。

PRONUNCIATION — Reduction of want to and have to. Listen and practise. 语音 —《want to 和 have to 的弱读》。先听录音跟读。 p. 109

{{AUDIO:p109-t7-track-7.mp3|CD 3 · p. 109, first track}}

/wanə/   A: Do you want to go to dinner with me tonight?
/hæftə/   B: I'm sorry, but I can't. I have to study for a test.

口语里这两个词组会连读：
want to → /wanə/（像「wanna」）
have to → /hæftə/（注意 have 里的 v 变成 f 的音，因为后面是清辅音 t）
have to 的这个 f 音最容易被忽略——读成 /hævtə/ 就不自然了。

  • 1want to is reduced to — /wanə/
  • 2have to is reduced to — /hæftə/
  • 3In have to, the v sound becomes — an f sound — /hæf-tə/

{{AUDIO:p109-t8-track-8.mp3|CD 3 · p. 109, second track}}

invitations · verb + to

Do you want to see a play with me tonight?
Sure. I'd really like to see a good play.
I'd like to (see a play), but I have to work late.		Would you like to go to an amusement park?
Yes, I'd love to (go to an amusement park)!
I'd like to (go), but I need to study.

I'd = I would

发出邀请两种说法：Do you want to…?（随意）／Would you like to…?（更客气）。
接受：Sure. / Yes, I'd love to.——注意 I'd 是 I would 的缩写，不是 I had。
婉拒的固定套路是先说想去，再给理由：I'd like to, but I have to / need to…
want、would like、have、need 后面都接 to + 动词原形，这是本单元的核心。

{{AUDIO:p017-ex03-grammar-focus.mp3|Track 33}}

negative statements · yes/no questions · short answers

I'm not from Rio.Are you from São Paulo? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
She's not from Japan.Is she from the U.S.? — Yes, she is. / No, she's not.
It's not English.Is it French? — Yes, it is. / No, it's not.
They're not in India.Are they in Egypt? — Yes, they are. / No, they're not.

一、否定就是在 be 后面加 not，不需要别的词。中文说「我不是里约人」，英语说 I'm not from Rio.
二、提问把 be 提到最前面：Are you…? Is she…? 中文靠「吗」，英语靠语序。
三、肯定简答不缩写：Yes, I am.（不说 Yes, I'm.）

GRAMMAR FOCUS — Wh-questions with be 语法重点 — be 动词的特殊疑问句 p. 20

{{AUDIO:p020-ex08-grammar-focus.mp3|Track 39}}

Wh-questions with be

What's your name?My name is Sophia.
Where are you from?I'm from Canada.
Who's that?She's my sister.
How old is she?She's twenty-eight.
What's she like?She's very nice.
Who are they?They're my classmates.
Who's = Who is

疑问词放句首，紧跟 be 动词，再放主语。中文的「什么、哪里、谁」留在原位，英语必须提前。
注意区分：What's she like? 问的是「她这个人怎么样」（性格），不是问长相，也不是问她喜欢什么。

GRAMMAR FOCUS — The future with be going to 语法重点 — 用 be going to 表示将来 p. 73

{{AUDIO:p073-t3-track-3.mp3|CD 2 · p. 73, first track}}

the future with be going to

Are you going to do anything this weekend?Yes, I am. I'm going to celebrate my birthday.
No, I'm not. I'm going to stay home.
Is Rosa going to cook dinner for you?Yes, she is. She's going to cook a special dinner.
No, she's not. She's going to order takeout.
Are your friends going to be there?Yes, they are. They're going to stop by after dinner.
No, they're not. They're going to be away all weekend.

be going to + 动词原形 ＝ 打算／将要做某事，说的是已经想好的计划。
结构是 am / is / are + going to + 动词原形——变化的是 be 动词，going to 永远不变。
疑问句把 be 提到前面：Are you going to…？ 否定在 be 后面加 not：I'm not going to…
中文靠「要、打算、会」表示将来，动词本身不变；英语必须把 be 动词选对。

PRONUNCIATION — Reduction of going to. Listen and practise. 语音 —《going to 的弱读》。先听录音跟读。 p. 73

{{AUDIO:p073-t4-track-4.mp3|CD 2 · p. 73, second track}}

A: Are you going to go to the game?   B: No. I'm going to study for a test.    →   /ɡənə/

说得快的时候，going to 会缩成 /ɡənə/（听起来像「格呢」），写出来常写作 gonna。
但只在讲计划的时候这样读，也就是 be going to + 动词。
如果 going to 后面跟的是地点名词（I'm going to a party. 我要去参加一个聚会）， 那是「去某地」的意思，不能弱读。
对比：I'm going to have a party.（要办聚会，可弱读）／ I'm going to a party.（要去聚会，不弱读）

  • 1In Are you going to go to the game? the words going to are pronounced — /ɡənə/
  • 2We use this reduction when we talk about — future plans — be going to + a verb
  • 3In I'm going to a party. (going to + a place) do we reduce it? — No — here going to means travelling to a place

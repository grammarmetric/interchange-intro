GRAMMAR FOCUS — Simple present Wh-questions 语法重点 — 一般现在时的特殊疑问句 p. 65

{{AUDIO:p065-t3-track-3.mp3|CD 2 · p. 65}}

simple present Wh-questions

What sports do you play?I play soccer and basketball.
Who do you play basketball with?I play with some friends from work.
Where do you play?We play at a gym near the office.
How often do you practice?We practice twice a week.
When do you practice?We practice on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
What time do you start?We start at six in the evening.

语序是固定的：疑问词 + do/does + 主语 + 动词原形？
What 什么 · Who 谁 · Where 哪里 · When 什么时候 · What time 几点 · How often 多久一次
When 问的是「哪天／哪个时段」，What time 问的是「几点钟」——中文都说「什么时候」，容易混。
跟中文最不一样的一点：疑问词放句首，而且后面一定要有 do/does，动词保持原形。

GRAMMAR FOCUS — Can for ability 语法重点 — 表示能力的 can p. 67

{{AUDIO:p067-t7-track-7.mp3|CD 2 · p. 67, first track}}

can / can't for ability

I / You / She / He / We / They can sing very well.Can she sing? — Yes, she can.
I / You / She / He / We / They can't sing at all.Can he sing? — No, he can't.
What can I do? — You can sing.Who can sing? — Becky can.

can ＝ 会／能。后面永远接动词原形，而且不随人称变化—— 没有 she cans，也没有 does she can。
否定是 can't（＝ cannot）；疑问句把 can 提到主语前面：Can she sing?
短答就是 Yes, she can. / No, he can't.——后面不用再重复动词。
can't … at all ＝ 完全不会；can … very well ＝ 很擅长。

PRONUNCIATION — Can and can't. Listen and practise. 语音 —《can 和 can't》。先听录音跟读。 p. 67

{{AUDIO:p067-t8-track-8.mp3|CD 2 · p. 67, second track}}

/kən/   I can play the piano.     /kænt/   I can't sing at all.

这是本课最容易听错的地方。
句子中间的 can 读得又轻又短，像「kən」（近似「肯」的轻声）；
can't 读得重而长，像「kænt」，元音是张开嘴的 /æ/。
所以分辨 can 和 can't，不能只听结尾的 t —— 要听元音和轻重。
但在简短回答里，can 要重读成 /kæn/：Yes, I can.

  • 1In I can play the piano, the word can is — short and weak — /kən/
  • 2In I can't sing at all, the word can't is — long and strong — /kænt/
  • 3In the short answer Yes, I can. the word can is pronounced — /kæn/ — short answers stress can

GRAMMAR FOCUS — There is, there are 语法重点 — there is / there are p. 47

{{AUDIO:p047-t7-track-7.mp3|CD 2 · p. 47, second track}}

there is · there are

There's a bed in the bedroom.There are some chairs in the kitchen.
There's no sofa in the bedroom.There are no chairs in the living room.
There isn't a table in the kitchen.There aren't any chairs in the living room.
There's = There is

中文说「房间里有一张床」，主语就是「房间」。英语要用一个不指任何东西的 there 开头， 真正的名词放在后面：There is a bed in the bedroom.
单数用 There is，复数用 There are——动词要跟着后面的名词变，不是跟着 there 变。
否定有两种写法：There isn't a table. = There's no table.

PRONUNCIATION — Words with th. Listen and notice /θ/ and /ð/. 语音 —《th 的两个读音》。听录音，注意 /θ/ 和 /ð/ 的区别。 p. 48

{{AUDIO:p048-t9-track-9.mp3|CD 2 · p. 48, first track}}

There are thirteen rooms in this house. The house has three bathrooms.

/ð/ is voiced — there, this, the. /θ/ is not — thirteen, three, bathrooms.

舌尖要轻轻放在上下牙之间。/ð/ 声带振动：there、this、the；/θ/ 声带不振动：thirteen、three、bathroom。
汉语没有这两个音，很多人会读成 s 或 z——记得让舌头露出来一点。

  • 1there — /ð/
  • 2thirteen — /θ/
  • 3this — /ð/
  • 4the — /ð/
  • 5three — /θ/
  • 6bathrooms — /θ/

Why two days per unit  为什么每单元用两天

Every unit in this book is built in two halves, called Cycle 1 and Cycle 2, each with its own conversation and its own grammar point. One cycle is one lesson. Nothing is set for home, so all the listening, the exercises and the speaking happen in the two hours.

本书每个单元都分成两半，叫 Cycle 1 和 Cycle 2，各有自己的对话和语法点。一个 cycle 就是一次课。因为不留家庭作业，所有听力、练习和口语都在这两小时里完成。

How each session runs每次课怎么上
Before we start 课前 — open the unit for today at grammarmetric.github.io/interchange-intro. Each file is 5–9 MB and takes a moment to arrive; after that it works with no internet.
打开今天的单元。每个文件 5–9 MB，加载需要一点时间；加载完就可以离线使用。
The language switch 语言开关 — the worksheet has an English | 中文 button. Read the instruction in 中文 whenever it helps, then switch back. The English dialogue, word banks and grammar boxes never disappear — that is the part you are learning.
练习页上有 English | 中文 按钮。看不懂说明就切到中文，然后切回英文。英文对话、词库和语法框始终都在——那才是要学的内容。
Answering 答题 — answers are chosen from a menu, not typed. You practise spelling out loud (Day 1), not on the keyboard.
答案是从下拉菜单里选，不用打字。拼写靠嘴巴练（第 1 天），不靠键盘。
At the end of every session 每次课结束时 — press Check answers to see your score, then Save file. Next time, press Load file to get your work back.
先点 Check answers 看分数，再点 Save file 保存。下次上课点 Load file 就能接着用。
Stuck? 卡住了？ — Show answers reveals the key. Use it after you have tried, not instead of trying.
Show answers 会显示答案。先自己做，做完再看，不要用它代替思考。

By Day 10 you can十天后你能做到
  • Introduce yourself, spell your name aloud, and exchange a phone number.
    自我介绍，大声拼出自己的名字，交换电话号码。
  • Name the things around you and say where they are.
    说出身边物品的名称，并说清楚它们在哪里。
  • Say where you are from, ask where someone else is from, and describe what they look like.
    说自己是哪里人，问别人是哪里人，并描述对方的长相。
  • Describe clothes and colors, and say whose something is.
    描述衣服和颜色，说清楚东西是谁的。
  • Tell the time, and say what someone is doing right now.
    说时间，说出某人此刻正在做什么。
  • Use be in statements, negatives, Yes/No questions and Wh-questions — the backbone of the next ten units.
    在陈述句、否定句、一般疑问句和特殊疑问句中使用 be 动词 —— 这是后面十个单元的骨架。
